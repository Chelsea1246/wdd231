Membership Levels

NP Membership

A no-fee membership designed for nonprofit organizations and community-focused groups.

Bronze Membership

A flexible membership for small and growing businesses looking to connect with the Accra business community.

Silver Membership

Additional opportunities for businesses seeking greater visibility, networking, and training.

Gold Membership

Enhanced benefits designed for businesses that want greater promotional and networking exposure.

NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to selected chamber resources.
  • Community event opportunities.
  • Networking with other organizations.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Access to networking events.
  • Business development opportunities.
  • Access to selected training programs.
  • Business listing in the chamber directory.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Additional training opportunities.
  • Increased business visibility.
  • Promotional opportunities.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Priority networking opportunities.
  • Enhanced promotional exposure.
  • Opportunities to showcase your business.
  • Greater access to chamber events and training.
